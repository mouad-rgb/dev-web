Our Games

League of Legends is a team-based strategy game where two teams of five champions battle to destroy the enemy Nexus. Choose from over 160 champions, each with their own abilities and playstyle, and take on a role such as top, jungle, mid, bot or support. Every match is different, mixing quick decisions, coordination and mastery of your champion. Play casually with friends or climb the ranked ladder and compete at the highest level.

Windows macOS

Valorant is a tactical 5v5 shooter where precise gunplay meets unique agent abilities. Each round, one team attacks and tries to plant the Spike while the other defends the site. Every agent brings their own tools, from smokes and flashes to traps and ultimates, so teamwork and smart strategy matter as much as aim. Win rounds, manage your economy, and be the first team to take the match.

Download:

Windows macOS

Teamfight Tactics is a strategy auto-battler where you face seven other players in a battle to be the last one standing. Recruit champions from a shared pool, combine them into powerful teams, and place them on the board to fight automatically. Each round you adapt to what you find in the shop, the items you receive and your opponents' choices. With new sets and mechanics regularly, no two games are ever the same.

Download:

Windows macOS

Wild Rift is the League of Legends experience rebuilt for mobile and console. You still play 5v5 matches to destroy the enemy Nexus, but with touch controls, a streamlined map and games that last around 15 to 20 minutes. It is perfect for quick sessions on the go while keeping the depth of champions, roles and teamwork. Play with friends anywhere and climb the ranks from your phone.
